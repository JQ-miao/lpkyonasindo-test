INDONESIA

Indonesia

Indonesia is located in Southeast Asia. Its new capital is Nusantara, and the relocation from Jakarta to East Kalimantan is underway.

Stretching about 5,110 km east to west, Indonesia is the world’s largest archipelagic nation with more than 17,000 islands, and is the only G20 member in ASEAN.

Japan and Indonesia continue to maintain strong relations, with many Japanese companies operating in Indonesia, making both countries important economic partners.

Japan and Indonesia continue to maintain strong relations. Today, many Japanese companies operate in Indonesia. According to JETRO, about 49.5% of companies plan further expansion, and 71.4% have already secured profitability.

In trade, Japan’s exports to Indonesia were about USD 12.98 billion (2024), while Indonesia’s exports to Japan were about USD 24.85 billion (2024). As a result, Japan is an important economic partner for Indonesia, ranking third as both an export and import destination.

— PRESIDENT'S MESSAGE

Integrity・Trust・Pioneering・Innovation・Excellence

Representative President

We sincerely thank you for your continued patronage and support.

Since our founding in 1998, our company has been engaged primarily in dispatching Indonesian human resources overseas. Thanks to your support, we have steadily grown for over 27 years and have developed into a highly specialized human resource service organization with international influence.

To date, we have received numerous awards and honors from the Government of Indonesia, and we have also obtained dual international quality management certifications based on ISO9001:2000. We are the only manpower dispatch company in Indonesia to hold ISO certification.

Since our establishment, we have dispatched approximately 50,000 people across a wide range of industries to countries and regions such as Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Canada, and Japan. In recent years, as economic ties between Indonesia and Japan have grown closer, we have been focusing more on staffing services for Japanese companies—dispatching outstanding talent to Japan and supporting Japanese-affiliated companies operating in Indonesia in securing personnel who can work smoothly in Japanese.

We provide consistent support from selection and pre-departure training to after-service upon return. We dispatch Indonesian talent to Japan who demonstrate strong learning ability, high professional skills, and excellent self-management. We sincerely hope they will grow through their experience in Japan, contribute to Indonesia’s development after returning home with their knowledge and skills, and become a bridge between Japan and Indonesia.

We will continue to strengthen our end-to-end system—from selection and training to post-arrival follow-up—and deliver greater value to both companies and talent.

INDONESIAN OVERSEAS WORKFORCE

Indonesian Overseas Workforce

In recent years, the number of Indonesian workers employed in Japan has increased rapidly.

According to Japan’s Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (published Jan 2025), the number of workers from Indonesia increased by about 48,000 from 2023 to 2024, showing one of the highest growth rates by nationality (after Vietnam).

Dispatch from Indonesia to Japan continues to grow year by year, with particularly strong expansion under the “Specified Skilled Worker” visa.

In addition, the Indonesian government has set a policy goal to expand the number of workers dispatched to Japan to 250,000 by 2025, and bilateral talent exchange is becoming increasingly active.
